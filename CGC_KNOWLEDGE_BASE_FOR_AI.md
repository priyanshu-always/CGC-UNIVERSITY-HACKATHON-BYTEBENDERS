## 6. Common Student Questions & How to Answer
- **Q: How can I take admission?**
  - **A:** Admissions for 2026-27 are currently open. You can apply online through our official portal. Our admissions team will assist you with document verification and seat confirmation.
- **Q: Is there any entrance exam?**
  - **A:** Direct admissions are open based on 12th board merit, or you can appear in CGC JOSH to secure up to a 100% scholarship.
- **Q: Are hostels compulsory?**
  - **A:** No, hostels are optional. Day scholars can easily use the university bus facility.

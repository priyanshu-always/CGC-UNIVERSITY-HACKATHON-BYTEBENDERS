### ⏱️ The 3-Minute Pitch Script for Judges

#### 1. Hook & The Real Problem (0:00 - 0:45)
> *"Respected Judges, during peak admissions (May to August), CGC University Jhanjeri receives over **25,000+ student enquiries**. A human counseling team can only dial 40-50 students a day. Students wait hours just to know basic fees, hostel rules, and placement stats. The result? 60% lead drop-off and massive counselor burnout.*
>
> *Today, we present **CGC VoiceGenie** — an autonomous AI Voice Calling Agent specifically architected for CGC University Jhanjeri."*

#### 2. Live Demo WOW Factor (0:45 - 2:00)
> *"Let me show you a real, live call right now. No fake mockups — let's call our AI counselor **Ritu** directly on screen."*
> 
> *(Click the glowing 'Talk to AI Counselor' button)*
> - **Question 1:** *"What is the tuition fee for B.Tech CSE?"*
>   - **Expected AI Response:** Mentions ₹65,000 - ₹75,000/sem and up to 100% CGC JOSH scholarship!
> - **Question 2 (The Hackathon Winner):** *"Hostel kaisa hai aur khana kaisa milta hai?"* (Speak in Hindi/Hinglish to show regional language understanding).
>   - **Expected AI Response:** Mentions separate AC/Non-AC hostels, 4 meals daily, 24/7 security.
> - **Question 3:** Interrupt the AI while it's speaking to demonstrate **Full-Duplex Barge-in**.

#### 3. Outbound Auto-Dialer & Telephony CRM (2:00 - 2:40)
> *(Switch to the **Telephony CRM** tab in the top navbar)*
> *"Our project doesn't just receive calls — it solves the outbound bottleneck. With our **Autonomous Auto-Dialer**, an admissions officer can click one button to dial hundreds of registered inquiries sequentially. The AI detects sentiment, qualifies candidates into **Hot, Warm, or Cold Leads**, and automatically syncs the transcript into our Supabase CRM."*

#### 4. The Tech Stack & Architecture (2:40 - 3:00)
> *"Our architecture integrates:
> 1. **OmniDimension Telephony Core (Agent #277505)** with sub-250ms voice latency.
> 2. **Sarvam AI (Ritu Neural TTS)** for natural Indian accent pronunciation.
> 3. **Soniox Fast STT** with barge-in interruption.
> 4. **CGC Jhanjeri RAG Knowledge Base** indexed with real fees, ₹1 Cr placement, NAAC A+ accreditation, and scholarships.
> 
> CGC VoiceGenie reduces inquiry handling costs by 85% while providing 24/7 instant counseling. Thank you!"*
